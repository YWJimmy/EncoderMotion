Two-pass validation
===================

Pass 1 - source and interface validation
- app_config.h retains the optimized button polarity and debounce definitions.
- APP_LEFT_ENCODER_SIGN is +1.
- APP_RIGHT_ENCODER_SIGN is -1.
- APP_LEFT_MOTOR_DIRECTION_SIGN is +1.
- APP_RIGHT_MOTOR_DIRECTION_SIGN is -1.
- motor.c and motor.h expose matching init/enable/disable/set/stop/brake/getter APIs.
- strict C11 compilation with -Wall -Wextra -Werror -pedantic passes.

Pass 2 - direction behavior validation
For logical motor_set(+300,+300):
- left: AIN1=HIGH, AIN2=LOW
- right: BIN1=LOW, BIN2=HIGH
- both PWM compare values are equal
- getters return +300,+300

For logical motor_set(-300,-300):
- left: AIN1=LOW, AIN2=HIGH
- right: BIN1=HIGH, BIN2=LOW
- getters return -300,-300

Forward encoder normalization:
- observed left forward count was negative; new left sign reverses it to positive
- observed right reverse count was positive; after motor reversal its raw direction
  reverses, and the new right sign converts forward travel to positive

Physical-board validation is still required after flashing.
