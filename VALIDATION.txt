EncoderMotion v4 validation plan

Static validation performed:
- Strict C11 syntax checks for motion.c, serial_log.c and empty.c.
- Integer ranges reviewed for speed x16, PI integral and synchronization terms.
- Conditional-integration anti-windup implemented at PWM limits.
- Encoder deltas are cleared at the start of every new motion.
- Forward and reverse motion normalize encoder speed to a positive direction.
- Left-ahead position error lowers left target speed and raises right target speed.
- Encoder-fault detection uses net directional progress rather than raw transitions.
- No blocking UART transmission was added to the 5 ms control task.

Target-board validation required:
- Confirm sl/sr follow tl/tr at PWM 300.
- Confirm no sustained PWM oscillation.
- Verify ENCFAULT by temporarily disconnecting one encoder ground at low risk with
  the wheels raised off the floor.
- Recheck 3000 mm distance and lateral deviation.

Host plant regression results:
- Normal 3000 mm run with the simulated right motor 5% stronger:
  state DONE, left count 21247, right count 21246, difference 1, ef=0.
- Frozen left encoder:
  state ENCFAULT after 42 control updates, ef=1, both PWM outputs zero.
