EncoderMotion v5 serial output additions

MOT,...,ef=<fault>,dt=<elapsed_ticks>,fg=<fault_grace_ticks>

dt:
  Number of real 5 ms SysTick periods represented by the latest control update.
  Usually 1. A value greater than 1 means the main loop was blocked and the controller
  normalized encoder speed using the actual elapsed time.

fg:
  Remaining startup-grace periods before encoder-stall monitoring becomes active.
  100 at motion start, then decreases to 0. One period is 5 ms.

SYS ov:
  Count of SysTick events that occurred while a control request was already pending.
  ov may increase during slow OLED software-I2C service. v5 no longer replays those
  missed periods as zero-time motion updates.
